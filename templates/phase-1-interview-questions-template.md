## Interview Questions by Section

### 1) Business Context and Strategic Goal

- What business result matters most in the next 12 months?
- Why is this result urgent now?
- What has changed in your market that makes your current approach less reliable?
- Which customer segment is highest priority right now?
- What does success look like in specific operational terms?

### 2) Audience Beliefs and Lived Experience

- What does your prospect currently believe about why they are stuck?
- What language do they use when describing their situation?
- What frustrates them most about their current reality?
- What have they normalized that should not be normalized?
- What do they fear will happen if nothing changes?

### 3) Failed Alternatives (Note 2 Discovery)

- What have prospects already tried before engaging you?
- Why did those options fail in practice?
- Which alternatives look attractive but produce weak outcomes?
- What bad advice is common in your category?
- Which assumptions keep prospects repeating failed approaches?

### 4) Unique Approach Mechanics (Note 3 Discovery)

- What do you do differently at the mechanism level?
- What sequence do you follow that competitors usually skip?
- What principle explains why your method works?
- Which part of your approach is hardest to copy credibly?
- What proof can demonstrate mechanism, not just outcomes?

### 5) Trust and Offer (Note 4 Discovery)

- Why should a skeptical buyer trust you now?
- What specific deliverables do clients receive?
- What evidence shows your process works repeatedly?
- What third-party validation exists (testimonials, references, credentials)?
- What objections appear right before purchase decisions?

### 6) Transformation and Outcomes (Note 5 Discovery)

- What changes in the first 30, 60, and 90 days?
- What longer-term transformation should clients expect?
- Which identity shift occurs when clients succeed?
- What specific behaviors stop after transformation?
- What new behaviors become normal?

### 7) Evidence Inventory (Reasoning, Story, Trust)

- What data or logic supports your claims? (Reasoning)
- What client stories best represent the shift? (Story)
- What credibility assets can we cite? (Trust)
- Where are proof gaps right now?
- Which missing proof is highest priority to collect?

### 8) Diagnostics (Hype, Data, Crown, Structure)

- Do current materials lean hype-heavy, data-heavy, or argument-centered?
- Do your materials mostly describe what is, or clearly state what should happen?
- Do you lead with recommendation, then argument, then evidence?
- Where are buyers left to connect the dots themselves?
- Which claim is currently under-crowned by evidence?

## Output Format

Produce:

1. Interview question list grouped by section.
2. One-line rationale for each section.
3. Priority interview order (what to ask first).
4. Top 5 critical questions if session time is limited.

## Completion Checklist

- Questions are specific, not generic.
- Questions are mapped to note development needs.
- Questions can surface major premise, minor premise, and conclusion.
- Diagnostics for hype/data and Crown Your Facts are included.
- Output is ready to use in a live client interview.
