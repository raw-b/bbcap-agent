# Billy Broas' Syllogism Framework

## Overview

Billy Broas now requires a syllogism as the **upstream foundation before all marketing work**, including before touching the Five Lightbulbs framework. As of October 2025, he states: "I refuse to start a marketing project unless we have a solid syllogism in place."

## The Syllogism Structure

Based on Aristotelian logic, adapted for marketing:

**Major Premise:** A foundational belief your audience must hold (or come to hold)  
**Minor Premise:** Your specific claim, built on that foundational belief  
**Conclusion:** What the audience should now believe or do, if the above premises are true

## Position in Marketing Hierarchy

The syllogism is the **upstream structure that makes everything else work**. It precedes:
- Five Lightbulbs framework application
- Content strategy development
- Sales page copywriting
- All marketing messaging

## Core Principle: Prove the Premises

Once the syllogism is established, the marketing job becomes elegantly simple: **prove the premises**.

All content, messaging, and marketing material should anchor to proving one or both premises. This transforms content creation from "endless random ideas" to "systematic proof-building."

## Billy's Beer Brewing Example

**Major Premise:** You can't improve what you can't accurately perceive

**Minor Premise:** Developing your palate helps you detect flaws and identify desirable flavors worth replicating

**Conclusion:** A trained palate becomes the brewer's most powerful tool for making better, more intentional beer

From this tiny structured argument, Billy created endless marketing material—all focused on proving these two premises.

## Nested Arguments

Arguments often contain **nested sub-arguments** within premises. When building a systematic case, you'll discover that some premises reveal their own supporting arguments that must be unpacked.

This is normal and expected. As Billy notes: "This isn't a quick exercise. Building a defensible argument takes hard thinking."

## Relationship to Five Lightbulbs

The Five Lightbulbs framework now operates **in service of proving the syllogism's premises**:

- **Lightbulb 1 (Status Quo):** Often proves why the major premise matters
- **Lightbulb 2 (Other Options):** Invalidates alternative approaches to the problem
- **Lightbulb 3 (Your Approach):** Proves your minor premise
- **Lightbulb 4 (Your Offer):** Delivers on the conclusion
- **Lightbulb 5 (New Life):** Shows the conclusion's transformation

## Process Integration

**Step 1:** Develop the syllogism (major premise, minor premise, conclusion)  
**Step 2:** Identify what evidence/proof is needed for each premise  
**Step 3:** Apply Five Lightbulbs framework to organize proof systematically  
**Step 4:** Create content that proves premises across all marketing channels

## Key Quote

"Everything downstream depends on what you build upstream."
