# **Billy Broas' 5 Lightbulbs Framework for ADHD Treatment Marketing**

Billy Broas' 5 Lightbulbs Messaging Framework represents a fundamental shift from feature-focused marketing to **belief-building messaging** that guides prospects through their emotional journey to purchase. For ADHD treatment programs, this framework addresses the unique challenges of overcoming stigma, building trust, and transforming skeptical prospects into committed patients through systematic belief establishment.

## **The core framework philosophy**

The 5 Lightbulbs Framework operates on the principle that **customers need specific beliefs to embrace your product**—without these beliefs, no amount of discounts, testimonials, or tactics will suffice. Broas uses the metaphor of a "Bridge of Transformation" where prospects stand on one side of a steep cliff feeling uncertain, and each lightbulb illuminates critical beliefs needed to reach safety on the other side.

Unlike traditional marketing funnels that assume linear progression, this framework acknowledges that **customer decision-making is non-linear and emotional**. Each lightbulb represents a "container of messaging" that creates "aha moments"—when all five lightbulbs are activated, customers see you as their obvious and best option.

The framework addresses five distinct emotional states prospects experience: frustration with their current situation, skepticism from failed attempts, curiosity about new possibilities, evaluation of specific solutions, and readiness for transformation. **Most businesses get trapped in "Lightbulb 4 silo"** by only focusing on product features, missing the crucial belief-building steps that precede purchase decisions.

## **The five lightbulbs structure for ADHD treatment**

### **Lightbulb 1: Customer's status quo (unacceptable situation)**

**Purpose:** Language of empathy demonstrating deep understanding of their current struggle **Patient mindset:** "I'm living with ADHD symptoms, and I'm frustrated" **Writing prompt:** "We know it's hard..."

**For ADHD treatment programs:** Address the daily reality of attention difficulties, missed deadlines, relationship strain, and feeling like they're not reaching their potential. Research shows adults with ADHD experience poor self-worth, sensitivity to criticism, and increased self-criticism. **If you can describe their problems better than they can, they'll assume you have the solution.**

Key messaging: Focus on the emotional toll of executive function challenges, the exhaustion of masking symptoms, and the frustration of being misunderstood. Use specific language that resonates: "constantly distracted," "starting projects but never finishing," "feeling overwhelmed by simple tasks."

### **Lightbulb 2: Other things they've tried (failed solutions)**

**Purpose:** Acknowledge previous attempts and address skepticism **Patient mindset:** "I've tried managing this—nothing worked. I'm skeptical" **Writing prompt:** "You think your problem is X, but it's really Y"

**For ADHD programs:** Research reveals that over 50% of patients believe they've tried evidence-based therapies when they haven't, and many attempt self-help strategies that fail. Address common failed approaches: willpower-based solutions, generic productivity systems, or inadequate medical treatment.

Rather than ignoring alternatives, **intelligently acknowledge why previous attempts failed** while positioning your specialized approach. Frame the realization: "You think your problem is lack of discipline, but it's really about brain chemistry and executive function requiring specialized intervention."

### **Lightbulb 3: Your approach (new possibility)**

**Purpose:** Present your unique methodology or treatment philosophy **Patient mindset:** "Maybe there's a better way to manage ADHD?" **Key insight:** This represents your approach, not your product

**For ADHD treatment:** Introduce your evidence-based methodology—perhaps an integrated approach combining medication management, cognitive behavioral therapy, and executive function coaching. This is your "secret sauce" or signature system that differentiates you from generic mental health providers.

Emphasize specialized ADHD expertise, understanding of neurodevelopmental differences, and comprehensive treatment philosophy. **As markets mature, messaging must shift from promises to how promises are achieved**—explain your unique mechanism for ADHD treatment success.

### **Lightbulb 4: Your offer (treatment solution)**

**Purpose:** Present your complete treatment program details **Patient mindset:** "This looks promising—but is it right for me?" **Writing prompt:** "Here's everything you get..."

**For ADHD programs:** Detail your comprehensive offer including initial assessment, treatment planning, therapy sessions, medication management (if applicable), family education, and ongoing support. Include pricing, session frequency, expected timeline, and any guarantees or success metrics.

When other lightbulbs are properly addressed, less time needs to be spent here because prospects are already "pre-sold" on the approach. Focus on the complete treatment experience rather than just individual components.

### **Lightbulb 5: Customer's new life (transformation vision)**

**Purpose:** Paint a picture of life with effectively managed ADHD **Patient mindset:** "I trust you and your approach. Let's do this" **Writing prompt:** "Oh the places you'll go..."

**For ADHD treatment:** Describe sustained focus at work, improved relationships, better emotional regulation, and finally feeling like themselves. **Connect the dots between your treatment and lifestyle transformation**—many prospects can't automatically envision how clinical treatment translates to daily life improvements.

Use specific transformation language: "Imagine completing projects you start," "having energy left for your family after work," and "feeling confident in your abilities." Include testimonials showing real lifestyle changes, not just symptom reduction.

## **Belief identification for ADHD treatment marketing**

The framework's power lies in **systematic identification of required beliefs**. For ADHD treatment programs, prospects need to believe:

**Core beliefs to establish:**

* ADHD is a legitimate neurological condition requiring professional treatment  
* Evidence-based treatment produces measurable improvements when properly implemented  
* They deserve support and don't need to struggle alone  
* Recovery and life improvement are achievable with appropriate intervention  
* Your practice understands their specific challenges and has relevant expertise

**Research methodology for belief mapping:** Start with the foundational question: "What does my patient need to believe to seek treatment?" Conduct comprehensive customer interviews asking: "What led you to seek ADHD treatment?" and "What made this decision important for you?" Listen for recurring themes about barriers, motivations, and transformation expectations.

**Analyze testimonials strategically**—most testimonials only address Lightbulb 4 (treatment satisfaction). Collect stories addressing all five lightbulbs: initial struggles (LB1), failed self-management attempts (LB2), discovering your approach (LB3), treatment experience (LB4), and life transformation (LB5).

## **Implementation strategy for ADHD treatment programs**

**Sequential messaging approach:** Deploy content addressing each lightbulb progressively. Start with empathy-focused content (Lightbulb 1\) through blog posts about ADHD challenges, social media content acknowledging daily struggles, and awareness-stage advertising that simply says "we understand."

Progress to Lightbulb 2 content addressing skepticism through case studies of failed DIY approaches, articles explaining why willpower-based solutions don't work for ADHD, and testimonials from patients who tried multiple approaches before finding success.

**Multi-channel lightbulb integration:**

* **Educational content:** Deep-dive articles for each lightbulb stage  
* **Email sequences:** Progressive belief-building campaigns  
* **Social media:** Bite-sized lightbulb-focused posts  
* **Sales pages:** All five lightbulbs woven throughout patient journey  
* **Paid advertising:** Targeted messaging based on awareness level

**Team alignment framework:** Create shared language across your practice. Content team focuses on Lightbulb 1 (problem awareness), marketing addresses Lightbulbs 2-3 (trust rebuilding and solution education), while clinical staff emphasizes Lightbulbs 4-5 (treatment details and transformation vision).

## **Advanced applications for healthcare marketing**

**Address healthcare-specific challenges:** The framework particularly suits ADHD treatment marketing because it systematically addresses stigma (LB1), failed previous attempts (LB2), treatment skepticism (LB3), and transformation possibilities (LB5). Research shows 83.1% of healthcare professionals report patient communication needs are unmet—this framework provides structure for meaningful patient engagement.

**Regulatory compliance integration:** The framework naturally supports compliant healthcare marketing by focusing on education and belief-building rather than making direct medical claims. Each lightbulb can contain evidence-based information about ADHD and treatment approaches without promising specific outcomes.

**Family-centered messaging:** ADHD treatment often involves multiple decision-makers (parents, patients, partners). Develop lightbulb content addressing different family member perspectives and concerns, recognizing that each may be at different belief stages simultaneously.

## **Measurement and optimization**

**Track progression through lightbulb stages** using engagement metrics, monitoring conversion rates at each transition point, and assessing message resonance through patient feedback. Measure long-term treatment adherence and satisfaction as indicators of successful belief establishment.

**Test different lightbulb focuses** to identify your market's "80/20"—which lightbulb resonates most strongly with your ADHD patient population. Some markets respond strongly to empathy (LB1), others to methodology explanation (LB3), and optimization requires systematic testing to discover your audience's primary drivers.

## **Conclusion and immediate next steps**

The 5 Lightbulbs Framework transforms ADHD treatment marketing from feature-focused clinical descriptions to comprehensive belief-building that addresses the emotional journey patients experience. **Success requires systematic customer research, strategic content development addressing all five emotional states, and continuous optimization** based on patient feedback and engagement data.

Begin implementation by conducting patient interviews to map current beliefs versus required beliefs for treatment engagement. Audit existing marketing content to identify which lightbulbs are currently addressed and which require development. Focus first on strengthening Lightbulb 1 (empathy) and Lightbulb 3 (unique approach) content, as these are typically underutilized in healthcare marketing while being critical for patient engagement and differentiation in the ADHD treatment space.
