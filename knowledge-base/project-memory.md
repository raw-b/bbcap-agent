Purpose & context
Robby is a marketing consultant and messaging strategist who runs his own consulting business serving SMB clients. His work centers on helping clients discover and articulate their core marketing argument — his signature deliverable is the Core Message Blueprint, a structured belief-building document built on his proprietary 5-Note Melody Framework (his client-facing adaptation of Billy Broas' Five Lightbulbs methodology). The five notes are: Empathy ("We Get You") → Broken Alternatives ("Those Aren't Best") → Unique Approach ("Our Way Is Best") → Trust & Offer ("You Can Trust Us") → Transformation ("We Know What You Want").
Terminology distinction: Billy Broas' constellation is the underlying methodology; Robby's Blueprint is the client deliverable. Use "blueprint" (not "constellation") in all client-facing and deliverable contexts.
Robby's ideal client is an SMB owner who senses their marketing pieces are disconnected but can't articulate why. His foundational pillar is radical empathy, and his primary diagnostic process is the LUCID 2-Day Strategy Sprint (a $5,000–7,500 service). He has completed 15+ formal strategy sprints across industries including medical practices, software companies, B2B services, and retail. His background spans roughly 20 years across ministry, consulting, and music composition.

Key learnings & principles

Expertise blindness: Clients' mastery of what they do prevents them from seeing what prospects need to hear — this is the root cause Robby's process diagnoses.
"Exhale moments": The highest-value outcome of a LUCID Sprint is naming what clients haven't been able to articulate themselves.
Argument before message: Following Billy Broas' evolved methodology, syllogism development comes first; the Five Notes are a proof organization tool, not a starting point.
Blueprint completeness scoring: Robby evaluates each Note on a 1–100% scale; Note 4 (Trust/Offer) is consistently the weakest across clients due to insufficient transformation stories and third-party validation.
Deconfliction discipline: Proof points must be mapped to specific pages/contexts to avoid redundancy across a client's full content ecosystem.
Staying risk vs. switching risk: For legacy modernization contexts, reframing "staying is the highest-risk option" outperforms minimizing switching risk.
Companion toolkit value: The five-piece activation toolkit (Beliefs Transformation Map, Gap Checklist, One-Page Core Message, Content Starter List, Email Sequence) makes the Blueprint actionable and is worth standardizing.


Approach & patterns

Robby operates a multi-Claude-Project architecture: separate specialized Claude Projects for each client (COO, Web Developer, etc.) with handoff briefs coordinating work between them. The main Claude instance synthesizes strategy; specialist projects execute implementation.
Deliverable format discipline: Copy-ready language only, Robby's own terminology preserved, maximum two options per element only when genuine alternatives exist, no blending between sections, no preamble.
QA loop: Completed Blueprints are tested against original interview transcripts in a COO Claude Project before finalization.
Voice preservation: Strong emphasis on authentic client voice; references a "Disallowed Words & Phrases" document to filter AI-generated language patterns.
Interview-first process: All Blueprint work begins with a structured Core Message Interview mapped to the 5-Note framework before any argument construction.
Version control: Blueprint documents are versioned (e.g., v1.3.0) and maintained across project iterations.
