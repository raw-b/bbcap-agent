# Creative Brief: Dr. Husayn's Integrated ADHD Program (REVISED)
## Five Lightbulbs Core Message Development

### CLIENT OVERVIEW
**Dr. Husayn Aly** - Sydney-based psychiatrist launching Australia's first fully integrated ADHD treatment program, positioning as the new standard of care rather than optional add-on.

### TARGET AUDIENCE
- **Primary:** High-achieving ADHD patients (C-suite executives, professionals)
- **Beta Phase:** 3-10 existing stabilized patients paying premium for validation 
- **Future State:** All new patients - this becomes Dr. Husayn's standard treatment protocol
- **Profile:** Adults seeking optimization beyond basic medication management

### PRODUCT POSITIONING
**"This Is How I Treat ADHD - Period"**
- Not a program or course - this is Dr. Husayn's methodology
- 12-14 month comprehensive treatment integrating psychiatrist, psychologist, dietitian, counselor
- First-of-its-kind in Australia, establishing new treatment category
- Future vision: No traditional "medication-only" option available

### PRICING STRATEGY & VALUE ARCHITECTURE
- **Full Price:** $12,000+ (anchoring for premium positioning)
- **Beta Price:** $6,500-7,000 (substantial discount for validation)
- **Out-of-Pocket:** As low as $3,000-4,000 after Medicare rebates
- **Comparison Context:** $600/month business coaching benchmark establishes value perception
- **Payment Plans:** 3-6 month options with slight premium for extended terms
